Planning your Phuket tour

Not sure what to book? Here is a short guide to our Phuket tours, from island day trips by speedboat to elephant care, rafting and sightseeing on land, with prices and the best time of year to go.

Which Phuket tour is right for you?

Most people start with a boat trip. If you want beaches and snorkelling, go for an island day tour to Phi Phi, the Khai Islands or James Bond Island. If you would rather stay on land, we also run elephant care, bamboo rafting, ATV rides, ziplining and sightseeing tours. Our join-in tours are the best value, and a private tour or charter gives you your own boat and your own pace, which works well for families and groups.

The most popular tours and what you see

Phi Phi, Maya Bay and the Khai Islands are our most popular snorkelling day trips by speedboat. James Bond Island and Phang Nga Bay mix limestone cliffs with sea canoeing. The Similan and Surin Islands are open from about mid-October to mid-May and have some of the best snorkelling in Thailand. Coral Island and Racha Island are closer to Phuket and good for a relaxed swim, and on land you can visit the Green Elephant Sanctuary, go bamboo rafting or try the Hanuman World zipline.

Phuket tour prices and what is included

Island speedboat day tours start from around ฿1,500 to ฿1,900 per adult, and premium boats and Similan trips start from about ฿2,700. Most tours include hotel pickup and drop-off in the main beach areas, national park and pier fees where they apply, lunch, snorkelling gear and insurance. Every tour page shows the live price and exactly what is included, and you can pay cash on the day.

The best time to visit Phuket for tours

November to April is the high season. The sea is calm and all the island trips run, so it is the best time for snorkelling and for the Similan Islands. May to October is the green season, which is quieter and cheaper, and most island tours still run with sunshine in between the showers. The weather at sea can change from day to day, so we check every departure before you travel.

How booking, pickup and payment work

Booking online takes a minute and you get instant confirmation. Most tours include hotel pickup, and we send you the pickup time after you book. You do not have to pay in full in advance, as you can pay cash to your guide on the day. We are a local team with Tourism Business Licence 11/12868, we run join-in and private tours every day, and you can reach us on WhatsApp or live chat if you would like help choosing.
