Chiang Mai Elephant Camp, Bamboo Raft & Hill Tribes Day Tour

Overview

A full day from Chiang Mai city hotels: elephant camp safari vibes, bamboo rafting on the Mae Taeng river, an art stop, and unhurried hill-tribe villages including Long Neck communities.

Highlights

  • Hotel pickup in Chiang Mai city
  • Elephant camp experience & show
  • Bamboo rafting through teak forest
  • Hill-tribe villages (Long Neck & more)
  • Northern lunch included

Itinerary

  1. 08:00 Hotel pickup (Chiang Mai city)
  2. 09:15 Elephant camp — care, show & ride experience
  3. 11:00 Ox-cart return to base camp
  4. 12:30 Northern lunch
  5. 13:30 Bamboo rafting
  6. 14:30 Art gallery
  7. 15:30 Hill-tribe villages
  8. 17:30 Return to hotel

What's included

  • Hotel transfers (Chiang Mai city)
  • Entrance fees & national park fees in program
  • Drinks, lunch, activities
  • Thai/English guide
  • Travel accident insurance

Meeting point

Hotel pickup inside the zones listed in the program around Chiang Mai. Pickup time is confirmed the day before.

Frequently asked

Where does the tour start?

Hotel pickup is included for the zones listed in the program around Chiang Mai. We confirm your pickup time the day before.

How do I pay?

Tell our staff the program, travel date and number of guests. After we confirm availability, the deposit is transferred to บริษัท ดีแอนด์จี ฮอลิเดย์ (ประเทศไทย) จำกัด only and you receive a deposit receipt. The balance is paid 20–30 days before departure.

Do you need my passport?

For packages we ask for your passport page (valid more than 6 months). If a visa is needed, our staff explains the steps.

What happens before departure?

3–5 days before the trip you receive the appointment sheet. 1 day before, the tour leader calls to introduce themselves.

Can the program change?

Yes — order of stops can change for weather, sea conditions or safety. We always keep the highlights listed above.
