Phu Chi Fa Day Tour from Chiang Rai

ทัวร์ภูชี้ฟ้า 1 วัน (ภูหลงถัง–ภูชี้ฟ้า–ภูชี้ดาว–ภูชี้เดือน)

Full day (~8.5 hours)Day trip · Mountains · Chiang Rai

OverviewItineraryWhat's includedFAQReviews

Overview

Climb the Chiang Rai highland ridge to Phu Chi Fa (1,628 m) with optional 4WD visits to Phu Chi Dao and Phu Chi Duean for Thailand–Laos panoramic views.

Highlights

  • Phu Chi Fa cliff viewpoint
  • Optional Phu Chi Dao & Phu Chi Duean 4WD
  • Lunch included
  • Private van / SUV options

Itinerary

  1. 08:30 Hotel pickup in Chiang Rai
  2. 11:00 Phu Chi Fa viewpoint
  3. 12:00 Lunch · continue to Phu Chi Dao (local 4WD extra)
  4. 14:00 Phu Chi Duean (local 4WD extra)
  5. 17:00 Return to hotel

What's included

  • Van/SUV, fuel, driver & Thai guide
  • 1 lunch
  • Travel insurance (as operator terms)

Meeting point

Hotel pickup inside the zones listed in the program around Chiang Rai. Pickup time is confirmed the day before.

Frequently asked

Where does the tour start?

Hotel pickup is included for the zones listed in the program around Chiang Rai. We confirm your pickup time the day before.

How do I pay?

Tell our staff the program, travel date and number of guests. After we confirm availability, the deposit is transferred to บริษัท ดีแอนด์จี ฮอลิเดย์ (ประเทศไทย) จำกัด only and you receive a deposit receipt. The balance is paid 20–30 days before departure.

Do you need my passport?

For packages we ask for your passport page (valid more than 6 months). If a visa is needed, our staff explains the steps.

What happens before departure?

3–5 days before the trip you receive the appointment sheet. 1 day before, the tour leader calls to introduce themselves.

Can the program change?

Yes — order of stops can change for weather, sea conditions or safety. We always keep the highlights listed above.
