FAQs

For lunch you can expect a delicious Thai style buffet at Phi Phi Don. You’ll have several choices like a curry, Pad Thai, stir fried, chicken & eggs and a dessert. The food is halal. If you have any specific food allergies, please let us know prior to booking this tour.

Our speed boat tours are not recommended for individuals who are over 70 years of age, pregnant women, children under 1 year of age, and those with recent surgery. If you have health conditions, we advise you to contact us before booking or consult with your doctor to ensure it’s safe for you to participate.

Safety is a top priority for us. The tour includes accident insurance, and our experienced guides and crew will provide a safety instruction session before departure. Life jackets are also provided for all guests.

The best time to visit the Phi Phi Islands is during the dry season, which typically runs from November to April. During these months, the weather is generally sunny with clear skies, calm seas, and mild temperatures, making it ideal for outdoor activities like swimming, snorkeling, and beach hopping.

Maya Bay closes each year for a few months during rainy season between 1 August and 30 September. We won't be able to dock in the bay to give nature a chance to restore itself. Please note that closing months vary each year, but we'll update our website accordingly.
